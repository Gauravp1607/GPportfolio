About Gaurav

Curious by nature.

I’m pursuing an MSc in Data Science and Big Data Analytics at MIT World Peace University in Pune. My projects explore practical AI/ML, from audio feature extraction and deep-learning workflows for Indian classical music to NLP/LLM assistants connected to APIs.

With HarmoLyric AI, I explore audio features and deep-learning approaches to recognize musical notes and connect them to Indian classical Sargam. QueryGenie gives me a complementary challenge: connecting natural-language questions to an LLM through an API-based assistant.

I also work with relational data using SQL joins, CTEs, window functions, indexing, and query optimization. I enjoy building queries that are efficient and easy to follow, then turning the results into clear, useful insights.

I’m aiming to grow into an AI/ML or SQL-focused engineering role, where I can keep learning and help turn data-heavy problems into dependable tools.

Outside tech, I enjoy Indian classical music and percussion, including pakhawaj and tabla.
